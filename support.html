Your first wall

  1. Create a plan. Measure the clear wall width and its height from the floor.
  2. Choose a center height for the entire group and a gap between frames. Leave clearance above furniture.
  3. Enter every frame’s outer width and height, including its border. Assign rows from top to bottom. Frame order within a row is left to right.
  4. Measure the actual hanging hardware: contact drop from the top, and separation for two contacts.
  5. Review the scaled plan. Start hanging to see each contact measured from the wall’s left edge and the floor.
  6. Check with paper templates, adjust for your hooks, then mark and hang. Check off each frame as you finish.
A contact point is not always a nail hole.
The calculated point is where the frame’s hanger rests on the support. A picture hook’s nail hole may be above that point. Account for the hardware offset before drilling.

How should I measure wire?

Pull it upward as it will sit on the real supports, then measure vertically from the frame top to the contact point. With two supports, use the actual separation while measuring. The app does not estimate wire sag, stretch or asymmetric hardware.

Which hangers work?

One centered contact, or two symmetric contacts at the same height. Off-center or uneven hangers, tilted frames and sloping floors need a manual layout. Check wall material, hidden utilities and hardware load ratings separately.

Why can’t I save?

Read the validation message below the frame list. Frames must fit on the wall; dimensions must be positive; contact drop cannot exceed frame height. Choose 1–12 frames in up to four rows. Names must be 1–80 characters.

How are rows spaced?

Each row is centered horizontally. Frames in a row align by their centers. Vertical spacing is the gap between adjacent row bounding boxes, each set by its tallest frame.

What units are supported?

Inches, including entries such as 12 3/8, and centimeters. Coordinates round to 1/16 inch or 0.1 centimeter. Internal dimensions are preserved when you switch units.

Why did checkmarks reset?

Geometry changes invalidate old marks, so progress clears when dimensions, hardware or layout change. Renaming a plan or switching units preserves progress.

Backups and moving phones

Choose Settings → Export all plans and save the JSON file. Import it on another device. Imports add copies without replacing existing plans. PDF guides cannot be imported. Export before deleting the app.

Is there a subscription?

No. HangLedger is designed as a one-time paid download with all features included. Apple manages purchases and refunds. Planning, saving and exporting work offline; external sharing may need a connection.
