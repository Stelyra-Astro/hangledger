Privacy policy

Your plans stay yours.

Effective October 14, 2026. HangLedger is provided by KUN WANG. Contact: Stelyra-Astro@proton.me.

Data in the app

HangLedger stores plan names, wall and frame measurements, frame names, modification dates, and installation progress locally on your device. We do not collect or receive this data. The app has no accounts, analytics SDKs, advertising, tracking, AI requests or application server.

Backups and exports

You can export a PDF hanging guide or a JSON backup and choose its destination with iOS sharing. Files sent to other apps or services are handled under their policies. Device backups may include HangLedger data according to your Apple backup settings. The app does not independently synchronize projects to us.

Purchases and Apple services

Apple processes the paid download and handles payment information under its privacy policy. We do not receive payment card details. Apple may provide sales or diagnostic reports according to your settings and Apple’s developer services. HangLedger does not include its own analytics or crash-reporting service.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you send. We use them to answer your request and troubleshoot the issue, not for advertising. Do not include private plans unless necessary. We delete support messages when no longer needed and generally within 12 months of resolving a request, unless retention is necessary for an ongoing dispute or legal obligation. You can request earlier deletion by email.

This website

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no analytics, advertising scripts, forms or cookies added by us. GitHub may process technical connection information, including IP addresses, to operate and protect its hosting service. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Your choices

Delete individual plans in the app, or delete the app to remove local app data. Delete exported files and device backups separately. For access, correction or deletion requests concerning emails you sent us, contact the address above. We cannot access the plans stored only on your device.

Changes

If these practices change, we will update this page and its effective date. The in-app policy describes the app’s local-data behavior.
